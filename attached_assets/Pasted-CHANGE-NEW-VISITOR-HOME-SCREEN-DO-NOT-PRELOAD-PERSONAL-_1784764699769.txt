CHANGE NEW-VISITOR HOME SCREEN — DO NOT PRELOAD PERSONAL HABITS

For users who have never used Me Next Level and are not signed in, remove the current pre-populated Assets and Liabilities from the live home screen.

Do NOT show example habits such as:

Morning Workout
Read 20 Pages
Meditation
Cold Shower
Tomorrow's Me
Skip Workout
Mindless Scroll
Late Nights

These examples are causing users to think Me Next Level is prescribing these behaviours to them.

Me Next Level must immediately feel personal.

New empty state

Under ASSETS — TEAM ME NEXT LEVEL, instead of example habits show:

BUILD YOUR TEAM

What will move you closer to your next level?

+ ADD MY FIRST ASSET

Under LIABILITIES — TEAM HOLDING ME BACK, show:

WHAT'S HOLDING YOU BACK?

Choose something you want to beat, reduce or stop.

+ ADD MY FIRST LIABILITY

When they click Add Asset

First ask:

WHAT MOVES YOU FORWARD?

Input:

Type your own...

Then underneath, optionally show:

Need inspiration?

Suggestions can appear as selectable examples such as:

Workout · Reading · Family Time · Saving Money · Building My Business · Healthy Eating · Learning

These are suggestions only. They must NOT automatically become part of the user's game.

When they click Add Liability

Ask:

WHAT'S HOLDING YOU BACK?

Input:

Type your own...

Then optionally:

Need inspiration?

Procrastination · Overspending · Self-Doubt · Too Much Screen Time · Poor Sleep · Negative Thinking

Again, these are suggestions only and must never automatically appear as the user's Liabilities.

First-time experience

Guide the user through creating:

1 Asset + 1 Liability

Then immediately return them to the arena and teach them how to take their first shot.

Do not make this feel like account setup or a questionnaire.

It should feel like they are building their teams before their first game.

After their first shot, they can continue playing and be encouraged to:

SIGN UP FREE — SAVE YOUR GAME

Existing users

IMPORTANT: Do not delete or alter Assets or Liabilities belonging to existing users.

This change applies only to the default experience for brand-new visitors/users who have not created their own.

Core principle

Never assume what somebody's goals or bad habits are.

The user's Assets and Liabilities define their personal game.
