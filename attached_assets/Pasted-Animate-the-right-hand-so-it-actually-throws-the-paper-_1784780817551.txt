Animate the right hand so it actually throws the paper.

The current first-person hands are realistic, but the right hand stays mostly static while the paper launches. I want the throwing motion to feel physical and believable.

Throw sequence
Ready position
Right hand grips the crumpled paper naturally.
Left hand stays open as a guide.
Pull back
When the user drags/pulls back, move the right hand slightly backward and downward.
Fingers tighten slightly around the paper.
Wrist rotates naturally.
Left hand can move subtly as if helping aim.
Release
When the user lets go, animate the right hand quickly forward.
Fingers open as the paper leaves the hand.
The paper must detach at the exact moment the fingers release it.
Follow-through
Right hand continues slightly forward/upward after release.
Fingers remain open briefly.
Wrist naturally follows the shot direction.
Then the hand returns smoothly to the ready position.
Paper flight
After leaving the fingertips, the paper follows the existing curved shot trajectory toward the hoop.
Do not make the paper teleport from the hand.
Important

The paper and hand animation must be synchronised:

grip → pull back → forward throw → fingers open → paper releases → hand follows through

The hand should not continue holding a duplicate paper after the shot begins.

Realism

Keep the motion subtle and fast, approximately:

pull-back: 200–350ms
release/throw: 150–250ms
follow-through: 250–400ms
return to ready: 350–500ms

Avoid exaggerated cartoon movement.

Mobile touch

The throw animation must be tied to the user's actual flick gesture:

stronger/faster flick = slightly faster hand release
weaker flick = softer release

Do not change the existing shot scoring logic.

Visual layering

Before release:

right hand in front/around paper

At release:

paper separates from fingertips

After release:

paper moves independently toward hoop

Do not leave another paper inside the hand.

Acceptance criteria
The right hand visibly throws the paper.
Fingers open at release.
Paper leaves from the fingertips.
Hand performs a natural follow-through.
No duplicate paper appears.
No duplicated hands appear.
Works on desktop mouse drag and mobile touch flick.
Existing hoop/net/score mechanics remain unchanged.

Do not redesign the arena or hands. Only animate the existing realistic hand assets/components to create the physical throwing motion.

This is one of those details that can make the whole game suddenly feel much more premium and game-like.
