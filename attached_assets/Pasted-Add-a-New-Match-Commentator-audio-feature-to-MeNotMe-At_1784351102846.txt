Add a New Match Commentator audio feature to MeNotMe.

At the beginning of each new daily game, introduce the match with a professional, energetic basketball-arena-style commentator voice.

The commentator should use dynamic game data where available, including:

User's first name
Current streak
Yesterday's result
Team Me vs Team Not Me
Important milestone proximity

Example new-user introduction:

“Ladies and gentlemen, welcome to the arena! It's Team Me versus Team Not Me. A new day. A new game. Let's play!”

Example returning user:

“Welcome back to the arena! Team Me is on a seven-day winning streak. But Team Not Me is back for another shot. The scoreboard is reset. Let's play!”

Example after yesterday's loss:

“A tough result yesterday, but today the scoreboard is back to zero. Team Me versus Team Not Me. A new game starts now!”

Example approaching a milestone:

“This is a big one! Team Me is one victory away from the Championship. The arena is ready. Let's play!”

Then trigger the arena sequence:

Commentator introduction → TUM-TA-TUM-TUM-TA arena rhythm → Fans cheer → arena lights/neon court pulse → scoreboard visually resets to 0–0 → NEW DAY. NEW GAME. → gameplay begins.

Keep the complete sequence short, ideally 5–8 seconds maximum, with a Skip Intro option.

Do not play the same commentary every day. Create multiple variations and select them based on the actual game state.

Add settings:

Commentator On/Off
Game Intro Sound On/Off
Crowd Audio On/Off
Master Sound On/Off

The commentator must never announce private Asset names, Liability names, Fan names or Doubter names without explicit user permission.

Use an original or properly licensed/generated commentator voice suitable for commercial use. Do not imitate or clone the voice of a real sports commentator.

Cache/preload common commentary audio where possible so the game introduction starts quickly.

For personalised dynamic commentary, use an approved text-to-speech system with a consistent fictional MeNotMe commentator voice.

Do not change the existing arena design or gameplay while implementing this feature.
