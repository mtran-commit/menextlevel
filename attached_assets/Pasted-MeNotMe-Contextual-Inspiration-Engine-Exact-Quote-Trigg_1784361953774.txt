MeNotMe — Contextual Inspiration Engine: Exact Quote & Trigger Rules

Implement an automatic Contextual Inspiration Engine.

There is no Inspire Me button. The system automatically selects an appropriate quote based on what is happening in the user's game.

Use the following original MeNotMe quotes and trigger rules.

1. NEW DAY / NEW MATCH

Trigger once when the user enters a new daily match, after Yesterday's Result and before normal gameplay begins.

Rotate between:

"Every day starts at zero. What happens next is yours."

"Yesterday's score is final. Today's game is not."

"Your streak starts with today."

"You don't become Team Me tomorrow. You choose Team Me today."

"One decision. One point. One step closer to who you want to become."

Sequence:

Yesterday's Result → Commentator introduces new match → one New Day quote if selected → arena drum rhythm → scoreboard 0–0 → NEW DAY. NEW GAME.

Do not always play a motivational quote during the intro. Randomise it so approximately 30–40% of new matches receive one.

2. TEAM ME IS LOSING

Trigger when Team Not Me leads by at least 2 points and sufficient time has passed since the previous inspirational message.

Rotate:

"The Final Bell hasn't rung yet."

"The score can change with your very next move."

"If Team Not Me scored, score back."

"A bad first quarter doesn't decide the final score."

"You don't need a perfect game. You need one more point than Team Not Me."

Do not trigger immediately after Team Not Me scores. Wait approximately 5–10 seconds so the quote feels motivational rather than intrusive.

Maximum once per losing period.

3. TEAM ME FALLS BEHIND AFTER LEADING

Trigger when Team Me was previously leading and Team Not Me takes the lead.

Rotate:

"The comeback starts with the next decision."

"The game isn't over."

"The score can change with your very next move."

"Back on the court."

Give this trigger slightly higher priority than the normal "Team Me is losing" trigger.

4. MULTIPLE MISSED SHOTS

Trigger after 2–3 consecutive missed Asset shots.

Rotate:

"Missed the shot? Pick up the paper and shoot again."

"Take the shot."

"Keep playing."

"One more point."

"The next point is yours to win."

Do not trigger after every miss.

Reset the miss counter after a successful basket.

5. SUCCESSFUL COMEBACK

Trigger when Team Me was losing and then ties or takes the lead.

Rotate:

"This is your comeback."

"Every time you choose differently, you change the score."

"One more point."

"Keep playing."

Fans should become more energetic when this quote plays.

Doubters should become quieter temporarily.

6. TEAM ME TAKES A STRONG LEAD

Trigger when Team Me leads by 3 or more points.

Rotate:

"Small wins become winning streaks."

"Build the streak."

"Finish strong."

"Make today count."

Do not trigger repeatedly as the lead increases.

Maximum once per daily game.

7. FINAL BELL APPROACHING — TEAM ME LOSING

This is a high-priority trigger.

When Final Bell is approaching and Team Me is behind, play one of:

"The Final Bell hasn't rung yet."

"The comeback starts with the next decision."

"The clock is still running."

"If Team Not Me scored, score back."

"Your move."

The commentator voice should become slightly more intense.

Crowd ambience can increase subtly after the quote.

Do not interrupt an active shot.

8. FINAL BELL APPROACHING — CLOSE GAME

If the difference is only 1 point near the Final Bell:

Rotate:

"One decision can change the game."

"One more point."

"The next point is yours to win."

"Finish strong."

"Your move."

Use a more dramatic but short delivery.

9. TEAM ME WINS THE DAY

After the Final Bell confirms a Team Me victory, rotate:

"You didn't just win the game. You showed up for yourself."

"Small wins become winning streaks."

"The person you want to become is built one day at a time."

"Every time you choose differently, you change the score."

"Make today count. Then do it again tomorrow."

Fans celebrate after the narration.

Do not play the quote over the main victory sound. Sequence:

Final Bell → Result → Crowd Celebration → Audio dips → Quote → Streak update.

10. TEAM NOT ME WINS THE DAY

After Team Not Me wins:

Rotate:

"Yesterday's score is final. Today's game is not."

"One day won't define you. What you repeatedly choose might."

"The comeback starts with the next decision."

"You can't change yesterday's scoreboard. You can change today's."

"Back on the court."

The delivery should be encouraging, not sad or judgmental.

Do not play a boo sound over the motivational quote.

Sequence:

Final Bell → Result → Team Not Me reaction → short pause → Quote.

11. STREAK BREAKS

When a Team Not Me daily victory resets the user's streak:

Rotate:

"Your streak ended. Your story didn't."

"Day One isn't starting over. It's starting stronger."

"Yesterday's score is final. Today's game is not."

"The comeback starts with the next decision."

"Your next streak starts with your next win."

These streak-specific messages should have priority over general Team Not Me loss quotes.

12. 7-DAY STREAK APPROACHING

When the user is one daily win away from the 7-day milestone:

Rotate:

"One more win. Your fans are waiting."

"Seven days isn't luck. It's consistency."

"One more game. Finish strong."

Use the commentator voice.

Fans should react subtly after the quote.

13. 7-DAY STREAK ACHIEVED

During the paparazzi/autograph celebration:

Rotate:

"Seven days. Seven victories. Team Me is building something."

"Give your fans something to cheer about. You just did."

"Small wins become winning streaks."

Play only one quote during the ceremony.

14. 30-DAY CHAMPIONSHIP APPROACHING

One daily victory before the Championship:

Rotate:

"The Championship is one victory away."

"Thirty days started with one decision."

"The arena is watching. Finish the game."

The commentator should deliver this with increased excitement.

15. 30-DAY CHAMPIONSHIP ACHIEVED

During the Championship Cup ceremony:

Rotate:

"Thirty days. You didn't wait to become Team Me. You chose it every day."

"Discipline is showing up when motivation stays home."

"The person you want to become is built one day at a time."

Play after the Championship Cup is revealed.

Then trigger the existing requirement to add at least one new Asset and one new Liability for the next stage.

16. 60-DAY WALL OF FAME APPROACHING

One victory before Day 60:

Rotate:

"One more victory and your name goes on the Wall."

"Sixty days of choosing who you want to become."

"You've come too far to stop playing now."

17. 60-DAY WALL OF FAME ACHIEVED

During the Wall of Fame ceremony:

Rotate:

"This isn't motivation anymore. This is who you're becoming."

"Your habits are keeping score. Look at the scoreboard."

"Don't play against everyone else. Play against yesterday's you."

Fans should stand and applaud.

18. 90-DAY RETIREMENT APPROACHING

One victory away from completing the 90-day journey:

This should be one of the most dramatic inspirational moments.

Rotate:

"Ninety days started with one decision. One more game remains."

"The Final Bell is coming. Finish what you started."

"One more victory. Play for the person you've become."

The commentator introduces the quote.

Arena crowd intensity increases afterward.

19. 90-DAY RETIREMENT ACHIEVED

During the Retirement Ceremony:

Use one of:

"Ninety days ago, you started playing for the person you wanted to become. Look who showed up."

"You came here to change the score. You changed the player."

"Team Me wins more than a game today. Team Me wins a season."

This should be a special narration, separate from normal quotes.

Play only once per completed 90-day season.

20. LIABILITY SCORES REPEATEDLY

If Team Not Me receives multiple points from ignored or failed Liabilities:

Rotate:

"Don't give Team Not Me an easy point."

"Your habits are keeping score, even when you aren't."

"One decision can change the direction of the game."

Do not identify or speak the actual Liability name.

21. USER RETURNS AFTER MULTIPLE DAYS AWAY

On returning after 3 or more inactive days:

Rotate:

"Welcome back. The scoreboard is waiting."

"No explanations needed. Get back on the court."

"The comeback starts today."

"Team Me needs you on the court."

Do not shame the user for being absent.

22. FANS REACTION QUOTES

Occasionally, instead of the narrator, allow the Fans to shout short phrases:

"YOU GOT THIS!"

"TEAM ME!"

"ONE MORE!"

"KEEP GOING!"

"LET'S GO!"

"FINISH STRONG!"

These should sound like arena crowd shouts, not narrator quotes.

23. DOUBTER REACTIONS

Doubters can occasionally shout short challenges:

"YOU'LL MISS!"

"NOT TODAY!"

"PROVE IT!"

"LET'S SEE IT!"

Do not use Doubter audio during sensitive moments such as a broken streak or after a significant loss.

Doubter reactions should be playful competitive pressure, never abusive.

GLOBAL AUDIO RULES

Do not allow inspirational audio to become repetitive.

Maximum:

3 full narrated motivational quotes per daily game.

Milestone ceremony quotes do not count toward this limit.

Use a minimum cooldown of approximately 10 minutes between normal automatic narrated quotes.

High-priority events such as Final Bell and milestone ceremonies can override the cooldown.

Never interrupt:

Active paper flight
Swish animation
Final Bell sound
Commentator introduction
Milestone narration

Queue the quote and play it after the active audio event completes.

Track recently played quotes and avoid repeating the same quote within the user's last 10 inspirational messages.

If Master Audio = OFF, do not play narration.

If Inspirational Audio = OFF, show the quote visually only if Inspirational Text = ON.

Allow users to disable:

Inspirational Audio
Inspirational Text
Fan Voices
Doubter Voices
Commentator
IMPORTANT IMPLEMENTATION RULE

Use the exact trigger categories above. Do not allow an AI model to independently decide when to interrupt gameplay.

The game engine determines the trigger.

The quote engine selects an eligible quote from that trigger category.

The audio engine handles narration.

The system should feel responsive to the user's game, but never overwhelming.

All quotes listed above are intended as original MeNotMe copy for this implementation.
