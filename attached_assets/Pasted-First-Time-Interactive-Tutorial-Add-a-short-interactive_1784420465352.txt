First-Time Interactive Tutorial

Add a short interactive first-time tutorial that explains how MeNotMe works inside the existing arena.

Do not create a separate onboarding page.

Do not redesign the main game interface.

The tutorial should appear as an overlay on top of the existing game and highlight real parts of the interface step by step.

Core principle

The user should learn MeNotMe by actually playing their first point.

The tutorial must feel like the opening of a game, not like filling in a form.

1. When to show it

Show the tutorial only when:

the visitor is new
they have never completed the tutorial before

For guests, store tutorial completion locally.

For registered users, save tutorial completion to their account.

Returning users must not see it again automatically.

Add a permanent option in:

Profile → How to Play

so the tutorial can be replayed later.

Also provide:

Skip Tutorial

during the first-time experience.

2. Tutorial Step 1 — Welcome

Keep the arena visible in the background.

Dim the rest of the interface slightly.

Display:

WELCOME TO MENOTME

Text:

Every day is a game between who you want to become and what holds you back.

Button:

SHOW ME HOW

Do not navigate away from the game.

3. Tutorial Step 2 — Team Me

Highlight the Team Me side of the scoreboard.

Dim other areas.

Display:

THIS IS TEAM ME

Text:

Your Assets play for Team Me.

Positive actions like working out, reading and learning can score points for the person you want to become.

Button:

NEXT

4. Tutorial Step 3 — Team Not Me

Highlight Team Not Me.

Display:

THIS IS TEAM NOT ME

Text:

Your Liabilities play against you.

Habits and choices you want less of can score points for Team Not Me.

Button:

NEXT

5. Tutorial Step 4 — Choose Assets

Highlight the Assets panel.

Display:

BUILD YOUR TEAM ME

Text:

Choose at least 3 Assets.

Show these suggested Assets:

Workout
Read
Drink Water
Eat Healthy
Sleep Well
Quality Time
Work on My Goals

Also show:

+ Create My Own

The user must choose at least 3.

Selected Assets should immediately appear in the real Asset panel.

Do not create duplicate tags.

Button becomes active after 3 Assets are selected:

NEXT

6. Tutorial Step 5 — Choose Liabilities

Highlight the Liabilities panel.

Display:

CHOOSE YOUR OPPONENTS

Text:

Choose at least 3 Liabilities you want less of in your life.

Show these suggested Liabilities:

Smoking
Unnecessary Spending
Mindless Scrolling
Too Much Sugar
Self-Doubt
Negative Vibes
Procrastination

Also show:

+ Create My Own

Selected Liabilities should immediately appear in the real Liability panel.

Button becomes active after 3 are selected:

START MY FIRST GAME

7. Tutorial Step 6 — Complete an Asset

Return focus to the full arena.

Highlight one Asset tag.

Display:

DID YOU DO THIS TODAY?

Text:

Tap a completed Asset.

When the user taps it:

the Asset becomes the crumpled paper
the paper appears in the user's hands
the basket glows slightly

Then proceed automatically.

8. Tutorial Step 7 — Teach the shot

Highlight:

paper
hands
basket

Display:

PULL BACK. FLICK. SCORE.

Text:

Pull the paper back and release it toward the basket.

The user must perform the actual drag-and-flick gesture.

Do not automatically shoot for them.

Do not allow the tutorial to progress until the user attempts the shot.

9. First successful basket

If the user misses:

Display briefly:

MISS — TRY AGAIN

Allow unlimited retries.

Do not penalise Team Me during the tutorial.

When the user scores:

paper travels through the rim
net moves
paper disappears below the net
Team Me becomes 1
cheer sound plays
Fans react
confetti appears

Display:

YOU JUST SCORED FOR TEAM ME

Then:

Every decision changes the score.

Button:

KEEP GOING

10. Tutorial Step 8 — Explain Liabilities

Highlight the Liability panel.

Display:

BEAT TEAM NOT ME

Text:

Avoid a Liability and Team Me can score at the Final Bell.

If it happens, Team Not Me scores.

Ignore it completely and Team Not Me gets the point automatically.

Show the three states visually:

AVOIDED
HAPPENED
IGNORED

Button:

NEXT

11. Tutorial Step 9 — Explain Final Bell

Highlight the Final Bell timer.

Display:

THE FINAL BELL DECIDES THE DAY

Text:

At the end of the day, the final score determines who wins.

Win the day to build your Team Me streak.

Button:

NEXT

12. Tutorial Step 10 — Explain streaks

Highlight the Day Streak display.

Display:

WIN THE DAY. BUILD THE STREAK.

Text:

Team Me wins the day when it finishes with the higher score.

If Team Not Me wins the day, your streak returns to Day 1.

Do not suggest that one individual Liability point resets the streak.

Only the full daily result determines the streak.

Button:

LET'S PLAY

13. Final tutorial sequence

After the user taps LET'S PLAY:

The commentator says:

“The teams are ready. The arena is waiting. Welcome to your first game.”

Then play the original MeNotMe arena intro rhythm:

TUM — TA — TUM TUM — TA

Then:

arena lights pulse
court neon travels
Fans cheer
scoreboard is ready
normal gameplay begins

Keep the full final sequence short.

Approximately 4–7 seconds maximum.

Allow:

Skip Intro

if the user wants to begin immediately.

14. Tutorial visual behaviour

Use:

existing black background
white neon
existing typography
existing arena
existing glass UI

Do not introduce:

colourful tutorial cards
stock illustrations
cartoon characters
new visual themes

Use a subtle spotlight/highlight effect around the current UI element.

Dim non-relevant parts of the arena slightly.

Do not fully hide the court.

The user should always feel like they are still inside the arena.

15. Tutorial interaction rules

During each tutorial step:

only the relevant control should be active when appropriate
prevent accidental actions on unrelated controls
allow Skip Tutorial at all times
preserve any Assets or Liabilities already created
do not reset game data if tutorial is replayed

When replayed from Profile → How to Play:

use existing tags
do not require creating new Assets or Liabilities
demonstrate the relevant controls without changing the user's real score unless they explicitly choose to practise in Tutorial Mode
16. Practice mode for replayed tutorial

If the user replays How to Play later:

Use a temporary practice score.

Do not modify:

official daily score
streak
achievements
season
history

Label it:

PRACTICE MODE

When complete, return them to the real game unchanged.

17. Audio

Tutorial respects:

Master Audio On/Off
Commentator On/Off
Crowd Audio On/Off
Sound Effects On/Off

If Master Audio is Off:

show all tutorial text
do not play commentator or sound effects

Do not force audio on.

18. Mobile behaviour

Make sure tutorial overlays do not cover:

highlighted control
paper
basket
key information

Position tutorial messages dynamically depending on screen size.

Test:

320px
375px
390px
393px
430px
768px

The tutorial must work on real iPhone Safari with touch gestures.

Acceptance criteria

The tutorial is complete only when:

A new user sees the arena immediately.
The tutorial happens inside the arena.
Team Me and Team Not Me are explained.
The user chooses at least 3 Assets.
The user chooses at least 3 Liabilities.
The user physically makes their first paper shot.
A successful first shot gives the correct celebration.
Liability scoring is explained.
Final Bell is explained.
Streak rules are explained correctly.
Tutorial completion is saved.
Returning users skip it automatically.
The tutorial can be replayed from How to Play.
Replayed tutorials do not change official game data.
The existing premium arena design remains unchanged.
Critical instruction

Do not redesign the MeNotMe game to implement this tutorial.

The tutorial must be built as a guided interactive layer over the existing arena and real controls.
