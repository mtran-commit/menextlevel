Scoring rules

Assets:

The user selects an Asset tag after completing it.
The selected Asset becomes the paper ball.
The user throws it into the basket.
A successful shot gives Team Me one point.
A miss does not give a point and can be attempted again.

Liabilities:

Every Liability must be addressed each day.
Addressed and avoided: Team Me gets one point at the Final Bell.
Addressed but happened: Team Not Me gets one point.
Ignored and never tapped: Team Not Me automatically gets one point.

Streak:

The streak continues when Team Me wins the full day.
The streak resets to Day 1 only when Team Not Me wins the full day.
Individual Team Not Me points do not reset the streak when Team Me still wins.
